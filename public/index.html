Questions

Before you ask.

Why join the early list?

You get the registration link the moment it opens on OKX, and we help you get your account ready before then so you can start on day one.

Who can enter?

Anyone 18 or older, based in Lebanon, with a KYC-verified OKX account. Main accounts only, no sub-accounts.

I already have an OKX account.

Good. Pick "I have one" in the form. Some existing accounts need to be linked to the competition before they're eligible. We'll check yours with you before registration opens.

What can I win?

The 12 finalists compete for a 5,000 USDT prize pool at the Live Finals. During the Online League, 1,000 USDT in trading-bonus rewards is shared across the two leaderboards.

How are the 12 finalists chosen?

There are two live leaderboards, PnL and Trading Volume. Your rewards from both are added up, and the 12 highest totals qualify. You need a 50 USDT minimum deposit and 5,000 USDT minimum trading volume to rank. Wash trading and multiple accounts get you disqualified.

Where can I get help?

Join our Telegram community. We answer questions there and help you with your OKX account and KYC, before registration opens and during the competition.

What happens to my number?

We use it only to send you competition updates on WhatsApp. We don't sell or share it, and you can ask us to delete it any time.
